My Personality

Who I Am

I am a person who is working towards personal growth and improving myself. I believe that my personality influences how I interact with others, respond to challenges, and approach my education and future career. Understanding my strengths helps me recognize the areas where I can continue to develop.

My Personality Traits

1. Hardworking

I consider myself hardworking because I understand that achieving my goals requires effort, commitment, and consistency. As a Computing student at the University of Technology, Jamaica, I recognize that learning programming and understanding new concepts require practice and patience. This trait encourages me to put effort into my assignments, improve my skills, and continue learning even when a topic is challenging. In my future career, being hardworking will help me complete tasks responsibly and continue developing my knowledge of technology.

2. Determined

Being determined means that I try to remain focused on my goals, even when I encounter difficulties. Studying Computing requires me to solve problems, learn unfamiliar concepts, and keep practising when I do not understand something immediately. My determination encourages me to keep working towards completing my degree and improving my abilities. In the workplace, this quality can help me approach challenges patiently, look for solutions, and work towards achieving professional goals.

3. Creative

I value creativity because it allows me to think about different ways to approach a task or solve a problem. Computing gives me opportunities to use creativity when developing websites, organizing information, and thinking about how technology can make tasks easier. This quality can help me consider different solutions instead of relying on only one approach. In my future career, creativity will be useful when developing ideas, improving processes, and finding practical ways to meet users' needs.

My Hobbies and Interests

Learning About Technology

One of my interests is learning about technology and how it can be used to solve everyday problems. As a Computing student, I enjoy the opportunity to develop my knowledge of computers, programming, and website development. Exploring technology encourages me to think creatively and develop new skills that can benefit me in my studies and future career.

Listening to Music

Listening to music is another activity that I enjoy because it provides a way to relax and take a break from my daily responsibilities. It helps me create time for myself while maintaining a balance between my academic work and personal life.

Personal Development

I am interested in improving myself and learning new things. I believe that developing my knowledge, communication skills, and problem-solving abilities can help me become more confident and prepared for future opportunities.

My Hero or Mentor

My Mother

My mother is a role model I can look up to because mothers often play an important role in teaching their children valuable lessons about life. I admire the idea of showing determination, responsibility, and perseverance when working towards personal goals.

The values I associate with this role model encourage me to remain focused on my education and continue working towards my ambitions. As I pursue my Computing degree at the University of Technology, Jamaica, I recognize the importance of patience, commitment, and continuous learning. These qualities can help me grow personally and professionally.
